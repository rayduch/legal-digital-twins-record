# Note on batch 2 (added 21 October 2026)

**What happened.** The second week of the October sitting had two cases, Beaird v. United States (25-5343, argued
Tuesday 13 October) and Salazar v. Paramount Global (25-459, argued Wednesday 14 October). The packets were approved on
8 October (Beaird revised and re-approved on 11 October to state the alignment of the sides in the packet body). Both
runs were executed on 11 October and dropped in the project's run store with full logs and hashes. The filing on
FantasySCOTUS, scheduled for Sunday 11 October, did not take place; the project lead was in Hong Kong for the LLMSS
workshops and the step had no second owner. The omission was noticed on 21 October, after both arguments.

**What this means for the record.** Rule 1 of the scoring rule, fixed on 4 October, says that a sitting that is not
filed is not scored and that the record says which sittings were filed. Batch 2 is therefore recorded here as *run,
not filed, unscored*. Its predictions are published for transparency — the run folders carry provider batch ids and
time-stamps of 11 October, and every input and output is hashed in `sealed/` — but a hash held privately is not a
public time-stamp, and nothing published after argument counts as a prediction in this record.

**What the twins said.** Beaird: all nine twins for vacatur (petitioner), mean probability 0.76. Salazar: seven twins
for reversal (petitioner), Alito and Kavanaugh for affirmance, mean probability 0.61. They are listed in
`predictions_2026-10-11.csv` with the FantasySCOTUS entry each would have implied, marked `filed = no`.

**What changes.** From the November sitting the filing step has a named owner and a written confirmation (filing record
with screenshots in the run store, and the public record updated the same day); the run is not considered complete
until that confirmation exists.

Raymond Duch, 21 October 2026.
